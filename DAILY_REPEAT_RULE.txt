CHORE SCORE — DAILY REPEAT RULE

Every chore can be completed up to 5 times per calendar day.

Scoring:
- Each accepted completion = 1 point.
- Maximum per task per day = 5 points.
- 254 tasks × 5 = 1,270 maximum daily points.

Cooldown:
- A task cannot be checked again until at least 90 minutes after its previous accepted check.
- The app displays N/5 today and the remaining lock time.
- The lock is enforced from stored completion timestamps.

Daily rollover:
- Completion timestamps reset from the active checklist at the next local calendar day.
- The completed day's total is stored in score history.

Migration:
- Existing legacy true/false checks are preserved as one completion for that task.
- Existing history and high scores are preserved.
